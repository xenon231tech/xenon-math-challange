# Privacy Policy - Math Challenge

**Last updated:** May 2, 2026

## Introduction

Math Challenge ("we", "our", "the game") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## Data We Collect

### Automatically Collected
- Game scores and progress (temporary, not stored permanently)
- Proof generation timestamps (for Billions TEE verification)

### No Personal Data
We do NOT collect:
- ❌ Names
- ❌ Email addresses
- ❌ IP addresses
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Wallet addresses
- ❌ Browser fingerprints

## How We Use Data

Game progress data is only used to:
- Display your current score during gameplay
- Generate Billions TEE proofs
- Show final results

No data is stored on our servers. All game logic runs in your browser.

## Third Party Services

| Service | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Vercel | Hosting | None (static site) |
| Billions Network | TEE verification | Proof timestamps only |

## Cookies

This game does NOT use cookies or any tracking technologies.

## Children's Privacy

This game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Since the game is static, check this page for latest version.
